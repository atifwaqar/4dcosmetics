Shipping & Delivery

We aim to get your order to you quickly and safely, anywhere in Pakistan.

Standard Delivery

  • Flat rate: PKR 250 on all orders.
  • Coverage: Delivery within Pakistan only.
  • Timeline: 3–5 working days via trusted courier services.
  • Tracking: Once dispatched, you’ll receive an email with your tracking details so you can monitor your parcel.

Urgent / Same-Day (ISB/Rawalpindi)

  • For urgent deliveries within Islamabad/Rawalpindi, we can arrange riders via inDrive or Yango.
  • Charges: Actual inDrive/Yango fare is paid by the customer.

Support & Communication

Note: “Working days” refer to business days. Delivery times may vary due to courier operations, weather, or public holidays.
